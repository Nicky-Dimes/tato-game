# Asset sources and rights

| Asset | Where it is | Source | Rights |
|---|---|---|---|
| Art boards (8) | `assets/reference/` | made by the owner with their image tool, from their own designs | owner |
| Tato frames, creatures, bosses, items, pickups, power icons, buttons, cards, world kits | `game/assets/sprites/` | cut from the boards above by `tools/art/` | owner |
| Terrain tiles, parallax backgrounds | `game/assets/tiles/`, `game/assets/sprites/bg/` | generated by `tools/art/make_art.py` (this repo) | owner |
| App icon | `game/icon.svg`, `game/web/icon_*.png` | drawn for this project | owner |
| Sound effects, honks, music | `game/assets/audio/` | generated by `tools/audio/make_audio.mjs` (this repo) | owner |
| Fredoka font | `game/assets/fonts/Fredoka.ttf` | https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/fredoka | SIL OFL 1.1 (licence text alongside) |
| Godot Engine | not in the repo (export templates) | https://godotengine.org | MIT |

No stock art, no sampled audio, no third-party music. The owner should confirm the
terms of their image tool allow the intended (public / commercial) use of the boards
before publishing.
